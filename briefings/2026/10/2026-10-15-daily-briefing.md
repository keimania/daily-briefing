### 2. [Visa secures China card business green light](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48584/visa-secures-china-card-business-green-light?utm_medium=rssfinextra&utm_source=finextrafeed)

**Source:** Finextra

**Summary (EN):**
Visa has received regulatory approval from the People’s Bank of China (PBOC) to establish a domestic bank card clearing business within mainland China. This landmark decision marks a significant step in the ongoing, multi-year opening of China’s multi-trillion-dollar domestic payments and clearing ecosystem to foreign market operators. The license will permit Visa to process local currency (RMB) transactions directly, allowing it to compete alongside existing domestic giant China UnionPay and fellow international entrant Mastercard. However, Visa faces formidable structural challenges, including deeply entrenched digital mobile payment giants like Alipay and WeChat Pay, which handle the vast majority of consumer retail flow. Strategically, this entry opens cross-border settlement channels and establishes an essential clearing infrastructure footprint for global institutional card transactions.
