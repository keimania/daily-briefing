**Source:** Reuters

**Summary (EN):**
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has proposed a significant revamp of how derivatives expiry prices are calculated to prevent market manipulation. Currently, the settlement price of derivatives contracts is calculated as the average of the last 30 minutes of trading on the expiry day. SEBI has expressed concerns that this methodology is vulnerable to price distortions caused by sudden trading volume surges or deliberate manipulation by large market players. The regulator's new proposal aims to introduce a volume-weighted average price (VWAP) or an alternative mechanism spanning a longer duration to establish fairer pricing. If implemented, this reform is expected to bring greater stability to the Indian derivatives market, which has seen explosive retail participation and heightened volatility in recent years.
